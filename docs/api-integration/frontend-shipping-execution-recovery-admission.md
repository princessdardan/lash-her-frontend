# Shipment execution recovery admission

Status: disabled-by-default P2-00 source component. Live admission, pending frontend SQL and all six rollout capabilities remain disabled. P2-00 remains incomplete.

The authenticated GET export at `/api/cron/chitchats-shipping` acquires a third independent recovery entry after refund and preparation complete, before the worker's default configuration and job claims. The handler factory accepts an optional fourth dependency, defaulting to passthrough. Disabled shipping skips execution; an enabled empty worker still settles its own entry. Closing recovery between batches refuses the next entry without replaying earlier batches. New-work admission remains independent.

The entry covers the complete claimed batch, provider calls and reconciliation, shipment/job persistence, tracking events, financial adjustment/refund enqueueing, notification handoffs and policy-alert fan-out. Existing operation identities, business leases, retries, fencing and admission-off counters remain intact. Claimed receipts must match the locked job identities, payload/hash, lease, attempts, version and outcome-unknown state. Successful completion receipts bind the original operation as well as the requested terminal outcome and cleared lease. Shipment, quote, provider-intent, event, cleanup and financial receipts are checked against the requested mutation. Email handoffs verify the persisted key/kind/order and decrypted recipient/payload; suppressed inserts and mismatched conflicts cannot prove completion. Alerts await every concurrent enqueue, including when another recipient failed first.

All current claimed types are audited in the [effect audit](baselines/2026-11-05-p2-00-shipping-execution/effect-audit.json): `create`, `quote_refresh`, `purchase` (including flat draft creation and reconciliation), `tracking`, `refund`, `delete`, `cleanup`, legacy `notification`, and removed `replacement_prepare`/`address_replace`. Known provider IDs and supplied order references must match. A bounded/malformed provider search cannot prove absence. `refund_requested`, manual review, invalid/future tracking events and swallowed alert failures retain uncertainty. Legacy notification delegation has no durable handoff receipt and always remains uncertain. Unsupported operations remain dead-lettered. Funding reservation finalization is a retained vacuous helper with no database mutation. Every retried, dead-lettered or fenced result remains uncertain, irrespective of its receipt.

Enforced uncertainty returns sanitized `503`, `Cache-Control: no-store` and `Retry-After: 30` before PII redaction. All selected asynchronous work is awaited before settlement. Socket loss, actual admission-backend death and lost settlement acknowledgement cannot prove drain or trigger automatic replay. Receipt markers are no-ops outside admitted work; additional receipt reads preserve existing off-mode return values and counters. Direct helper/admin executions remain unqualified.

The [source-bound validation](baselines/2026-11-05-p2-00-shipping-execution/validation.json), inventories and classification record the tested source. Controlled tests cover authentication, closed and independent recovery, disabled/empty/off behavior, closure after preparation and during worker stages, uncertain counters and caught markers, socket loss and acknowledgement loss. The actual GET runs through a loopback HTTP bridge and verified TLS PostgreSQL with migrated synthetic business rows. Strict synthetic Chit Chats interception exercises every type and provider request identity. Real triggers suppress/change claim, intent, draft, quote, shipment, event, movement, completion, cleanup, refund/adjustment and email receipts. Tests cover provider ambiguity, reconciliation, closure while claims/provider/persistence/alerts are blocked, business lease expiry, ledger termination, failure while a later claimed job finishes and failed alerts with a blocked sibling. A separate eligible redaction canary proves uncertainty prevents the following stage; this does not qualify redaction itself.

These are owned-database/source checks with synthetic producer identities and provider responses. They do not qualify deployed Next.js/native scheduling, real Chit Chats/email/provider drain, independent executions, historical producers or the parent release/restoration coordinator. Exact-head CI remains required. Shipment PII redaction is the next separate item. No deployment, publication dispatch, live control change or admission/pending-SQL enablement accompanies this component.
