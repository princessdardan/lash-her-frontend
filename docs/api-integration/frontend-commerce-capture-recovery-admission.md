# Uncaptured-commerce payment recovery admission

Status: disabled-by-default P2-00 source component. Pending frontend SQL and live admission remain disabled; P2-00 remains incomplete.

The authenticated GET at `/api/admin/payment-reconciliation` supplies a separate recovery runner for the uncaptured-commerce capture batch. Admission precedes its bounded order query and remains active through every Square lookup/completion, local capture receipt or atomic uncollected quarantine/shipment hold/alert enqueue, and later order in the batch. Closing recovery refuses before the query. Closing only new work leaves this recovery available.

The service monitor, email retry and training-split reconciliation retain their sequence. Email retry already has its own admission boundary; the service monitor and training-split reconciliation remain unqualified. The selected batch starts after training-split reconciliation returns. Neither its database query nor its payment effects are covered by this component. A training-split exception retains its existing behavior and prevents the later capture batch. Direct helper callers still require their own boundary. The whole HTTP route remains `UNQUALIFIED_WORKER`.

Caught failures, unknown provider statuses, failed summaries, uncollected funds, missing local receipt targets, socket loss and lost settlement acknowledgements cannot establish completion. Later orders still finish before settlement. Refusal or uncertainty returns sanitized JSON `503` with `Cache-Control: no-store` and `Retry-After: 30`. With admission off, partial failures retain the prior logging and monitor-derived HTTP response.

The selected live payment adapter now checks the requested payment identity on lookup and completion, and requires `COMPLETED` on the capture acknowledgement before writing a captured receipt. This also corrects the previous false-capture behavior with admission off. A successful HTTP response alone cannot establish capture. The captured receipt checks that exactly one paid Square order/payment row was updated. Uncollected handling retains the existing transaction, fulfillment quarantine and alert idempotency keys; no payment creation or new retry key is introduced.

The [validation record](baselines/2026-11-04-p2-00-commerce-capture/validation.json), effect audit, generated source inventories and classifications bind the component to source. They cover authentication, five closure stages, later orders after failures, undefined rejections, unknown/uncollected states, disabled/empty batches, sibling isolation, provider identity/status mismatches, missing targets, socket loss, lost acknowledgement and production route wiring.

These fixtures do not prove a combined actual cron/TLS-ledger/business-database flow, real provider delivery, quarantine/outbox rollback on PostgreSQL, or runtime drain. Full exact-head CI remains required. Historical producer fencing/bootstrap, the service monitor and training-split worker, other writers and the parent release/restoration coordinator remain open. No live deployment, provider/database/credential/scheduler changes, admission/pending-SQL enablement or rollout-control changes are made.
