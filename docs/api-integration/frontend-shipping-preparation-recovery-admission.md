# Shipping preparation recovery admission

Status: disabled-by-default P2-00 source component. Live admission, pending frontend SQL and all six rollout capabilities remain disabled. P2-00 remains incomplete.

The authenticated GET export at `/api/cron/chitchats-shipping` acquires a separate recovery entry for shipping preparation after the queued product-refund entry completes. Admission precedes the shipping-enabled check, expired-quote selection/abandonment, cleanup-job inserts, polling selection and tracking-job enqueueing. Closing new work leaves recovery independent. Closing recovery between the refund and preparation entries refuses preparation even if the earlier refund settled; that refund is not replayed. A disabled shipping batch still completes its own entry without business reads or writes.

Detached quote candidates are locked before abandonment so missing update receipts cannot resemble an empty batch. Attached candidates retain their existing order/obligation/shipment lock and eligibility rechecks; ordinary concurrent-state skips remain benign. Both paths verify persisted cleanup jobs, including the existing idempotency key, shipment, operation type, payload hash and payload. Tracking enqueueing retains its existing time-bucket identity and idempotency-conflict checks; a changed stored payload marks uncertainty for both newly inserted and reused jobs. Missing expected receipts and query/enqueue failures cannot complete an enforced preparation entry. Enforced failures return sanitized `503`, `Cache-Control: no-store` and `Retry-After: 30`, before shipment execution or PII redaction.

Admission-off behavior preserves existing summaries, idempotency keys and incident-shaped exceptions. Receipt uncertainty markers are no-ops outside admitted work. Quote cleanup receipt reads and detached candidate locks are additional database operations; no automatic provider retry or cleanup-job replay is introduced. Preparation admission ends after all selected enqueue work and transaction completion. Shipment-job execution, its external Chit Chats effects and PII redaction remain outside this boundary. A completed preparation entry proves neither their drain nor whole-route drain. Direct helper/admin callers remain unqualified.
